Welcome to HealthMate AI

Understand common symptoms, get general self-care tips, and learn when to consult a healthcare professional. This app is a student hackathon project and does not provide medical diagnoses.

1. Select or type symptoms

Pick from common symptoms or add your own. Use the chips to quickly build a list.

How it works

  • Client-side only — nothing leaves your browser.
  • Simple rules create a risk indicator: Low, Moderate, or Urgent.
  • Provides general self-care suggestions and clear guidance on when to see a doctor.

Preventive health tips

  • Wash hands frequently and cover coughs/sneezes.
  • Stay up-to-date on vaccinations and annual check-ups.
  • Eat a balanced diet, stay hydrated, and get regular sleep.
  • Avoid smoking and limit alcohol.
  • Manage stress through exercise, breaks, and social support.

Emergency & Disclaimer

Not medical advice: HealthMate AI provides general information only and is not a substitute for professional medical care, diagnosis, or treatment. It does not diagnose conditions or replace a clinician.

In an emergency: If someone has chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, sudden weakness or numbness, loss of consciousness, severe bleeding, or any life-threatening condition — call your local emergency number immediately.
