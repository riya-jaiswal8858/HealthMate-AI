# 🩺 HealthMate AI

HealthMate AI is a beginner-friendly web application that provides general health information, symptom-based guidance, self-care suggestions, and basic health awareness.

## 🌟 Features

- 🔍 Symptom Analyzer
- 🤖 AI Health Assistant
- 📊 Risk Level Indicator
- 💡 Self-Care Suggestions
- 🏥 Guidance on when to consult a doctor
- 🌿 Preventive Health Tips
- 📱 Responsive and user-friendly design
- ⚠️ Emergency health disclaimer

## 🎯 Problem

People often search online for health-related information but may find confusing or unreliable information.

HealthMate AI provides simple and understandable general health guidance and helps users know when professional medical care may be needed.

## 💡 Solution

Users can enter their symptoms and receive:

- General health information
- Basic self-care guidance
- Risk-level indication
- Preventive health tips
- Guidance about consulting a healthcare professional

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

HealthMate AI provides general health information for educational and awareness purposes only.

It does not diagnose diseases, prescribe medicines, or replace professional medical advice.

In case of an emergency, users should immediately contact a healthcare professional or appropriate emergency services.
